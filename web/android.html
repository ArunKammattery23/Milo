Choose your appearance

In Milo, open Setup → Appearance to choose System, Light or Dark. System follows your phone. The web companion also offers theme choices in Settings.

Get started

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installation for the app opening this file. Do not bypass Play Protect or other security warnings.
  2. Open Milo. Comfort, Goals, Memories, and prepared lessons work without an AI account.
  3. Local AI is the default. Choose On-device AI in Setup, check your phone, and download Gemini Nano if supported. Once ready, text generation has no API fee and needs no key. Unsupported phones can still use offline tools.
  4. In Talk, tap Speak and review your words before sending. Optional Direct voice sends the transcript automatically and speaks a reply. Replies stream as they arrive.
  5. Cloud AI is optional: select Gemini, OpenAI API, Claude, or Custom API in Setup. Get a developer API key, paste it, review data/price notes and tap Save connection. Use Test saved connection to check it; that sends only a short hello and may use quota or incur charges.

If replies or sound do not work

For local mode, check model readiness in Setup; not every Android phone supports Gemini Nano. For cloud mode, save the key for the selected provider and use Test saved connection. Check the model ID, quota, region and provider credits. Never send us your API key. To check audio, enable Milo and tap Setup → Test speaker, increase media volume, and choose a supported spoken language. Android voice settings can install or select a text-to-speech voice.

Choose how Milo thinks

On-device AI needs a compatible phone, Android AICore and a one-time model download. It is best for short tasks; no web research or background AI is available in this mode. Device quotas, storage, battery and download data still apply. Gemini can research the web. OpenAI, Claude and compatible HTTPS APIs offer optional cloud chat; choose an accessible model for deeper learning. This app never automatically switches to a cloud or paid provider. ChatGPT and Claude app subscriptions do not supply API access.

Meet Milo’s expressions

Milo shows listening, thinking, speaking, comfort, calm, curiosity, celebration, playfulness, and concern. Open Setup → Try Milo’s expressions for a preview. Choose Gentle, Playful, or Straightforward to guide AI replies. Milo’s instructions support calm disagreement and rest, without punishment, demands for obedience, proof of love, or hitting. These are illustrated expressions and conversation settings—not human feelings or a newly trained model.

A simpler conversation

The updated Talk screen keeps your Milo artwork and gives messages more room. Speak and Send are the main actions. Tap ••• for voice options, web search, privacy or a fresh conversation. Need a starting point opens ideas. Use More in the bottom navigation for Comfort and Memories.

Milo above other apps

In Setup, tap Show floating Milo pet. Allow notifications and Display over other apps, then return to Milo. Drag the pet to move it, tap to open Milo, or long press to hide it. Hide floating pet in Setup, Hide pet in its notification, or Off removes it. This small pet shows expressions while you use other apps. It does not turn on your microphone or read your screen. Android may stop it to save battery; it never starts at reboot. This is an independent Android companion with simple illustrated poses, not a connection to ChatGPT Work Pets.

You control your pet

Background voice is optional: start a session in Setup, grant microphone and notification permissions, and use the ongoing notification’s Stop microphone button to end it. It is turn-by-turn, with the microphone paused during replies. It may stop after silence or Android restrictions. There is no wake word and it never starts automatically after reboot.

Off stops the floating pet, voice sessions and reminders. Clock alarms belong to your separate Clock app. Memories uses only photos you select; private photo copies stay on your phone and are never uploaded to the AI.

Free-tier limits and privacy

Milo has no subscription or shared developer AI key. Local text generation has no per-request API fee on supported devices. Your AI provider sets prices, quotas, region and age eligibility. The app cannot check billing status; a paid key can incur charges. There is a 30-attempt daily cloud limit in this prototype. Free forever, unlimited AI, and instant responses are not promised.

When you chat, cloud mode sends your message, recent conversation, chosen mood, interests, goals, pet name, language preferences, and chosen conversation style to the selected provider. Local text inference stays on the phone. Speech services may process audio remotely. Google’s free-service terms may allow submitted content to improve its products. Keep confidential details out. Local data can be erased in Setup; that does not erase data already processed by providers.

What still needs work

Google account sign-in, automatic calendar updates, scheduled email delivery, and a native iPhone app are not connected. Calendar and email actions open your phone’s apps for review. AI, real-phone voice, photos, and reminders still need end-to-end testing. This build is not verified for public release, clinical use, or worldwide scale.

Milo supports everyday wellbeing, not diagnosis or treatment. It can’t guarantee stress relief, happiness, health improvement, or business success. Stay connected to trusted people and seek qualified support when needed.
